# Repeat rounds and scope control

## When asked for more

Read the current feature/workflow inventory and prior decisions. Do not automatically
add two new global modes or make the panel larger. Seek a materially different job,
handoff, responsibility, failure case or non-adoption reason.

Common useful increments:
- A drafted request gains attributed replies and controlled review.
- A planning brief gains a comparison against later reported outcomes.
- A household summary gains a named audience and explicit comparison baseline.
- Free-text attachment names gain typed reference metadata and disclosure controls.

These are examples, not required verticals. The right next step may be a smaller
extension to an existing screen. If two authorized additions are feasible, build
them; do not turn every missing production dependency into a permission gate for
a clearly fictional prototype. If a proposed addition has no distinct job, explain
that and choose a better bounded candidate using the user's context.

## Decision record per round

Retain:
- What decision this round addressed and which inputs changed.
- New perspectives versus retained roles; actual review method and its limits.
- Need/friction, supporting evidence class and unresolved assumptions.
- Candidate decision: prototype, extend, defer or reject.
- Overlap with current flows; dissent and simpler alternatives.
- Prerequisites modeled fictionally versus capabilities actually observed.
- Implemented paths, checks performed, failures/limits and next real validation.

Do not silently recast a previously deferred idea as newly discovered. Explain what
new input or modeled prerequisite made it worth testing. Keep earlier rounds as
historical records, not live truth. Avoid copying stale counts or accumulated claims
into new artifacts without checking what the records actually represent.

## Stop adding and start observing when

The user still gets the requested result. Recommend a real-case comparison when
new lenses mainly repeat the same need, features require duplicate maintenance, or
reviewing the generated output may take longer than doing the original task.

A hypothesis can be useful enough to prototype without being validated. Conversely,
a functional prototype and simulated agreement do not establish willingness to pay,
retention, conversion, market size, financial ROI or product-market fit.

## Handoff prompts

Panel only:
“Use $panel-to-prototype to review this product with operator, buyer, recipient and
boundary perspectives. Preserve counterarguments. Deliver needs and candidate
experiments only; do not implement or contact anyone.”

Full round:
“Use $panel-to-prototype on this app and these source materials. Run a simulated
panel, select two distinct workflow experiments, and implement them locally using
the existing design system. Keep AI simulated and show source/review controls.”

Further round:
“Use $panel-to-prototype to inspect what we already built. Add perspectives only
where they expose a new need or objection. Extend or build two further flows and
retain the rationale, disagreements and verification limits.”
