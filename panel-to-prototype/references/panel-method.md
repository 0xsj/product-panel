# Panel design and evidence handling

## Start from a decision and a job

Useful decisions include which professional workflow to prototype, whether a partner
needs a full workspace or a scoped handoff, what causes abandonment, or whether a
proposed AI function removes work. Avoid a generic panel judging whether a product
is “good.”

Read only relevant supplied material and permitted product surfaces. Name the
limits of access: marketing site versus logged-in app, admin-only versus multiple
roles, free versus paid features, screenshots versus working flows. A button or
role selector does not prove backend enforcement. A competitor's category practice
does not establish a customer's unmet need.

Use small role coverage proportional to the problem. Cover operational work,
purchasing, receiving the output and at least one plausible reason not to adopt.
Honor an explicitly requested panel size, but distinguish adjacent role variants
from separate customer groups. More role cards do not create more observations.

## Role record

For each perspective, capture:
- Job, trigger and actual responsibility boundary.
- Operator/buyer/recipient/referrer/affected/boundary relationships, including overlap.
- Existing workaround and likely switching/duplicate-entry burden.
- Possible participation incentive; mark purchasing assumptions as hypotheses.
- Concrete objection or failure condition.
- A question for a real professional that could overturn the proposal.
- Source/evidence IDs, plus what remains unsupported.

Use role titles or fictional labels. Do not fabricate quotes or portray invented
professionals as interviewed customers. Their statements are analytical lenses.

## Critique a scenario, not a pitch

Give reviewers the same bounded evidence packet and a fictional scenario with
specific incomplete records, deadlines, handoffs and competing statements. Ask:

1. What would this role try to do first, and what information is missing?
2. Which proposed action would save a step? Which creates another record to maintain?
3. Would a current export/email/checklist/vendor already solve it?
4. What may this role decide, and what belongs to someone else?
5. Which uncertainty should stay visible instead of becoming a confident status?
6. What would make this role decline to participate or pay?
7. What is the smallest experiment that distinguishes these alternatives?

Do not supply an intended verdict. Ask for counterexamples and role disagreements.
If using multiple agents, vary bounded responsibilities rather than asking every
agent to confirm the same feature list. Record shared inputs and actual review
units. Never relabel a group of role lenses as independent customer interviews.

## Ground role claims without upgrading evidence

Prefer primary professional bodies, official provider processes, product manuals
and user-provided artifacts for specific facts. Observe host browsing requirements.
If browsing is unavailable or prohibited, keep unsupported role duties hypothetical
and list the needed source checks; do not create fake citations.

A source can support “providers ask for item dimensions” without supporting
“providers will subscribe to this workspace.” Preserve that distinction. Record
URL/title, date accessed when applicable, claim supported and relevant limitation.
Do not embed confidential customer material into a reusable skill or public artifact
merely because it was available during analysis.

## Synthesis

Map roles to shared customer jobs; keep materially different incentives separate.
A recurring operator may value reduced administration, a recipient may value a
usable submission, and a referrer may want no ongoing account at all.

For every selected candidate retain at least one objection, alternative and real
validation question. Do not manufacture disagreement where none was expressed;
record limited agreement as a shared hypothesis with its prerequisites.

Keep counts literal:
- “Eight role perspectives represented” describes eight role records.
- “Three review tracks evaluated the prototype” describes three actual tracks.
- Neither describes eight interviews or independent demand signals.

Real validation should compare work on a recent anonymized case: time preparing
and checking, corrections, repeated questions, duplicate entry and whether the
recipient can take a useful next step. Purchase intent and conversion need their
own evidence.
